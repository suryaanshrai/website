Writing its review long after I read it but I believe the play might've been good to watch. Reading the script, I did not felt the punch that the original series carried or that engaging feeling that even [[Hogwarts Legacy]] has. The main character was ridiculous in my opinion. And the overall plot was not very enjoyable as well. It felt like a drag of the original series, instead of something that was made with as much actual love, passion, and efforts that the world of Rowling demands.
