Not what i was expecting. It started out as a very cybersecurity guy focused series and I was expecting it to be something on the lines of [[Silicon Valley]], but this is way waaayyyy different. By season 2 its genre even shifts completely to something else. Hacking and all just seemed secondary by the end of the series. 

A good watch, but plot focus shifts too frequently if a linear story is more appealing to you. This is just a heads up, because these shifts might actually turn into something that takes the interest away.

However some special episodes were actually quite fun, like that special episode with 2000s TV serial vibes, the one with Acts, and the other one with the movie vibe.

All in all, it was good. An 8/10 for sure.
