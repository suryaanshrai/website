Timid, naked, wild lurked hiding

Troglodyte in rocky caves,

O'er the fields went nomad striding

And the fields he laid to waste.

Hunter with his spear and arrows

Dreadful through the forests tore,

Woe to those left by the billows

On that harsh and hostile shore!
<br>

From Olympus' heights descended,

Mother Ceres goes in quest

Of Persephone, the ravished,

Wild before her lies the earth.
<br>

Neither nourishment nor lodging

Is there for the goddess there;

And no sign of godly worship,

Shrine or temple anywhere.
<br>

Fruit of fields and sweet grape-clusters

Do not glisten at the feasts;

Only reek remains of corpses

On the bloodstained altar-piece.

And with sad eye now wherever

Ceres now her gaze unrolls -

In deep degradation foundered

Everywhere man she beholds!"

<br>

That from baseness' vile dominion

Man may rise to soul's rebirth,

He must join eternal union

With his ancient Mother Earth."
