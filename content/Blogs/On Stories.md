Stories have power. They carry something in them that facts and statements tend to miss. The major religions are centred around stories. Cultural shifts happen through stories. New thoughts and ideas are introduced and advertised through stories. Games, movies, plays, novels, even young children's science books are portrayed through stories. There's something deeply intriguing about a well-structured narrative that captures the idea or multiple ideas. 


Stories have always had a lot of my attention and good ones still make me wonder. 
Major ones, which define religions, do actually carry a lot of ideas about how to conduct one's own life and also a lot on the morality of the people following the stories which in turn has a very big role to play in the way the person and the people at large following those stories behave and act. So those stories do have an impact. 
But what about the other ones? The countless other ones that almost everyone indulges in some form or the other? Do they also impact us? Do they also create a difference in us? Do they affect the way we decide the good and the bad, the to-do and not-to-do of our lives?

It gets very confusing when we abandon all views and all the learned morality from stories to demarcate the good and the bad. Do you forgive the criminal or do you punish? Do you eat meat or do you not?

Obviously there's always personal choices, reasoning and decisions. Universal rules can only be made from universal facts. And when there's diversity in the individuals, there lives and there beliefs, then things more fundamental than these turn to be focus areas. 

Anyways, thats a bit off topic. For me, stories are an indulgence, something through which I rejuvenate, relax and chill. Through novels, games, movies, anime, anything that's got a good story I'll indulge in it.

Its also my beleif that by opening myself to many stories, from multiple authors, across multiple cultures and regions of the world, I open myself and my mind to as many of the messages that these stories carry as I can, thus refining my good/bad discrimination system in the brain to be a bit better. Maybe its also a lie that I am gloriously telling myself for indulging in entertainment and wasting my time more than I should (sometimes), but I do believe that stories carry weight. They are powerful and their significance has been and still is one of the most impactful things that we as humans cherish together. Its also one of the best ice breaking conversations, about the other person's favourite movies and series that are mutually appreciated.
