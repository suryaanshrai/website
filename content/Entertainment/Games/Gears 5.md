Can't believe that this game is from 2018. It's so beautiful, plays so smooth and the overall experience of it is just so fun. I am enjoying this game more because of the balance that it has brought on the table between story, gameplay, combat, characters, pacing, length, visuals and fights.

However, its worth mentioning that the story is not as rich as other games, its mostly fighting and didn't felt very attached to the characters as well, although they were good.

All in all it's a great game to play especially considering that you can play the main campaign in co-op itself (which was the reason why I got it in the first place).
