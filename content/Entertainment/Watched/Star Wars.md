The Original and Prequel Triology were good. Like real good. But the sequel trilogy made me turn off the TV. So, didn't finished that.

My recommendation - _watch the first 6 movies which were released in chronological order_, live in that space and enjoy it in your own mind a bit. Then, try the latest 3 movies, other spin off series like _The Mandalorian_ (I hear its good).

I myself haven't watched more than the first 6 yet.

Listing my watching order recommendation here-

- The Original Trilogy (Directed by George Lucas)
  - Star Wars: Episode IV – A New Hope (1977)
  - Star Wars: Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back (1980)
  - Star Wars: Episode VI – Return of the Jedi (1983)
- The Prequel trilogy
  - Star Wars: Episode I – The Phantom Menace (1999)
  - Star Wars: Episode II – Attack of the Clones (2002)
  - Star Wars: Episode III – Revenge of the Sith (2005)
- The Sequel Trilogy, Mandalorian or other things that the star wars world offers.

If you are really keen, the wikipedia page for it is a good place to know more about it as well.
