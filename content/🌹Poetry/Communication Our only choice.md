It's funny how delicate it is

The string of bonds that hold us together.

Words are the fiber that form their strands

Spun together with emotions,

Into something that you can sometimes understand.

  <br>

Expression and comprehension, 

That's all we have. 

That is all we can do,

To save ourselves, the company of a few.
